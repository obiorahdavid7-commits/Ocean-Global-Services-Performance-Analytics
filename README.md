4-Page Executive Power BI Dashboard analyzing 200 orders | ₦399.61M Gross | 12K Qty | 26 Products | 14 Cities

### 📊 Pages
1. **Ocean Global Services Executive Overview** - Monthly, Quarterly, Net vs Gross trend
2. **Ocean Global Services Regional Performance** - Top/Worst 5 Cities by Net, Gross, Orders
3. **Ocean Global Services Customer Performance** - 7 Customer Types, Top Customers by Qty
4. **Ocean Global Services Products Performance** - Top 10 SKUs, Categories (Doors ₦156M = 39%)

### 🔑 Key Insights
- Luxury Security Doors drive 39% Gross (₦156M) - core margin driver
- Individual customers = ₦149M Gross (37%) + 70 orders but ₦6.5M discount leak
- Top 3 SKUs = ₦93M concentration risk
- Tiles lead qty (Marble Tile 2.2K) but low Gross - upsell opportunity
- Worst city Kano: ₦17M Net, 10 orders - expansion gap
- Discount leader: Freestanding Bathtub ₦2.0M - needs cap policy
